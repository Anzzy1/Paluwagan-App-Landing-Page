Two ways to run it

Online with your barkada, or offline face to face.

Every group runs its paluwagan differently. Pick the mode that fits yours when you create a circle.

Everyone uses the app

Online paluwagan

For groups who live far from each other. Each member creates a free account, and the whole circle runs inside the app.

  • Friends join with an invite code, link or QR
  • Members upload receipts and confirm payments
  • Reminders and group chat for everyone
  • Pay through GCash, Maya or bank
Only the admin uses the app

Managed offline

For groups who meet in person. One admin runs the whole paluwagan on their phone, and collects and hands out the money face to face.

  • No account and no internet needed
  • Add members by name. They don't need the app
  • Tick off each hulog as it's handed over
  • See whose sahod is next; advance, delay or deduct

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Send us a message.

What is a paluwagan?

A rotating savings circle. Every member puts in the same amount each round, and one member takes home the whole pot. It repeats until everyone has had a turn.

What's the difference between online and offline?

Online is for groups who are far apart. Every member makes a free account, joins the circle, uploads receipts and gets reminders. Offline (managed) is for groups who meet in person. Only the admin needs the app. They add members by name, collect the hulog face to face and tick off who has paid. Offline circles are saved on the admin's phone and don't need internet.

Do all members need to download the app?

Only for online circles. In offline mode, just the admin needs it. Everyone else pays the admin in person, like a regular paluwagan.

Does the app hold or move my money?

No. Members pay each other directly through GCash, Maya, a bank or cash. Paluwagan only keeps the record: receipts, confirmations, reminders and history.

How do I know the draw is fair?

The order is locked in the moment the draw starts, so a game can't be replayed to change the result. Anyone can tap Verify to recompute the order on their own phone and confirm it matches.

When will the APK, Windows and iPhone apps be ready?

Android and Windows builds are in progress. The iPhone app will come later. The web app has every feature, so you can start today.

Is it free?

Yes, it's free during the beta.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Please tell us. Use the Feedback button in the corner and describe what you were doing and what went wrong.
